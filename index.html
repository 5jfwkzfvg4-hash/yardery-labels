First-time setup · Step 2 of 2

Connect the label printer

One device at a time. The printer can only be connected to one phone or tablet. If someone else is connected right now, you can't connect until they're done. Phones let go of the printer by themselves when the app is closed or untouched for 3 minutes, or they can tap ⚙ → Disconnect. Turning the printer off and on also frees it.

    You're all set, !

    Labels from this device will say BY . From now on, this device connects to the printer by itself when you open the app. Pick an item, set how many, and print.

    Connect the label printer

    Use the printing link

    The Claude app can't talk to Bluetooth printers. Printing works from the store's own printing link, opened in Chrome (Android) or Bluefy (iPad). This copy inside Claude is for looking up items only.

    Open the printing link

    Android tablet

    1. Turn the Zebra on. Turn Bluetooth on in the tablet's settings.
    2. Open the printing link in Chrome.
    3. Tap Connect printer at the top, pick DFJ235305204 (the Zebra ZD411) from the list, and tap Pair.

    iPad

    1. Install the free Bluefy – Web BLE Browser app from the App Store. Safari and Chrome on iPad can't reach Bluetooth printers from a web page.
    2. Open the printing link in Bluefy and add it to the Home Screen from Bluefy's menu.
    3. Tap Connect printer and pick DFJ235305204.

    If the printer doesn't show up

    1. Make sure no other tablet or phone (like the old Jolt tablet) is connected to it. The printer only talks to one at a time.
    2. Power the printer off and on, then try again.
    3. Labels print blank or skip? Open ⚙ and tap Calibrate with the 1×1 roll loaded.

    Labels creep lower or print across two labels (Zebra ZD411)

    1. Open ⚙ and check Label roll says "gap" (or "black mark" if the roll has black lines on the back).
    2. Open the printer. Push the roll guides in so they touch both edges of the 1" roll.
    3. Gap rolls: slide the sensor under the labels back to its default center spot, lined up with the middle of the roll. Black-mark rolls: line it up under the black mark instead.
    4. Close the lid. When the light is solid green, hold PAUSE and CANCEL together for 2 seconds. The printer feeds a few labels and measures them (SmartCal).
    5. Print 5 labels in a row. If they're all off by the same small amount, use Move down in ⚙ (use a minus number, like -8, to move the print up).
