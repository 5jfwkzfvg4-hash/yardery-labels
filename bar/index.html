Pop's Moonshine

$13

Glass Pint (16 oz)

Ingredients

  • 2 ozOle Smoky Strawberry moonshine
  • 0.5 ozsimple syrup
  • 0.5 ozStrawberry Real
  • 1lime wedge, squeezed
  • 8–10mint leaves
  • Topsoda

Build

  1. Add all ingredients except soda to a shaker.
  2. Shake vigorously 10–15 times.
  3. Dirty dump into a pint glass and top with soda.

Offer the upsell

  • Make it a double (extra moonshine)

Guest menu: Ole Smoky Strawberry Moonshine, fresh mint, strawberry purée, lime, sparkling soda

Lawn Chair Lemonade

$12

Glass Collins (12 oz)

Garnish

  • Lemon wedge

Ingredients

  • 1.5 oz44 North Huckleberry vodka
  • 1 ozlemon juice
  • 1 ozsimple syrup
  • 1 bspdefrosted IQF blueberries with juice (barspoon)
  • TopSprite

Build

  1. Add all ingredients except Sprite to a shaker.
  2. Shake with ice.
  3. Pour into a Collins glass.
  4. Top with Sprite.
  5. Garnish with a lemon wedge.

To confirm: New menu proof drops the Huckleberry name, the Sprite and the Jack Daniel's Blackberry +$1 swap.

Offer the upsell

  • Jack Daniel's Blackberry in place of vodka

Guest menu: 44 North Vodka, shaken with fresh blueberries & lemonade

Signature CocktailThe Yardery · Bar Spec

It's Just Peachy Sangria

$12
Single serving

Glass Pint (16 oz)

Garnish

  • Frozen (IQF) peach, sunk in the glass
  • Orange and cherry, picked

Ingredients

  • 4.25 ozWhite Zinfandel
  • 0.375 ozNew Amsterdam Peach vodka
  • 0.375 ozorange juice
  • 0.625 ozcranberry juice
  • 0.375 ozsimple syrup
  • TopSprite

Build

  1. Fill a pint glass with ice.
  2. Add the wine, peach vodka, OJ, cranberry and simple syrup (6 oz total). Stir briefly.
  3. Top with Sprite.
  4. Sink a frozen (IQF) peach into the glass.
  5. Garnish with a picked orange and cherry.

Batch mix · pre-shift

  • 3 LWhite Zinfandel (2 × 1.5 L bottles)
  • 8 ozNew Amsterdam Peach vodka
  • 8 ozorange juice
  • 16 ozcranberry juice
  • 8 ozsimple syrup

Makes about 23 drinks. Pour 6 oz of mix per glass, then finish steps 3–5.

Offer the upsell

  • Float 0.5 oz peach schnapps

Guest menu: New Amsterdam Peach Vodka, White Zinfandel, fresh citrus, juicy peaches, cranberry juice, OJ

The Yard Fashion

$14

Glass Rocks (9 oz)

Garnish

  • Orange rind, expressed and dropped in
  • Bourbon-soaked (“dirty”) cherry

Ingredients

  • 2 ozOld Forester bourbon
  • 2 dashesaromatic bitters
  • 0.5 ozclove simple syrup
  • 1dirty cherry
  • 1orange rind, expressed

Build

  1. In a rocks glass, express one orange rind and drop it in with a dirty cherry and ice.
  2. Fill a mixing glass halfway with ice.
  3. Add the Old Forester, clove simple syrup and bitters.
  4. Stir and strain over the fresh ice in the rocks glass.

Offer the upsell

  • Woodford Reserve or Maker's Mark in place of Old Forester
  • Rye Fashion: Bulleit Rye

Guest menu: Old Forester Bourbon, house-made clove syrup, aromatic bitters, fresh orange

Siren's Fizz

$14

Glass Collins (12 oz)

Garnish

  • Bourbon-soaked (“dirty”) cherry, dropped in
  • Maraschino cherry on top

Ingredients

  • 1.5 ozMermaid Gin
  • 0.75 ozsimple syrup
  • 0.75 ozlemon juice
  • Topsoda

Build

  1. Add ice, gin, simple syrup and lemon juice to a shaker.
  2. Shake and dirty dump; top with ice and fill with soda.
  3. Drop in a dirty cherry and top with a maraschino cherry.

Offer the upsell

  • Hendrick's Gin in place of Mermaid
  • Add 0.5 oz Bols Elderflower liqueur

Guest menu: Mermaid Gin, fresh lemon juice, simple syrup, sparkling soda

Real Good Margarita

$14

Glass Collins (12 oz), salt rim

Garnish

  • Salt rim
  • Lime wedge

Ingredients

  • Rimsalt
  • 1.5 ozHerradura Silver tequila
  • 0.75 ozlemon juice
  • 0.75 ozlime juice
  • 1 ozagave
  • 1lemon wedge, squeezed
  • 1lime wedge, squeezed

Build

  1. Rim a Collins glass with salt.
  2. Squeeze the lemon and lime wedges into an iced shaker.
  3. Add the remaining ingredients and shake.
  4. Dirty dump into the Collins glass.
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge.

Offer the upsell

  • Top shelf: Casamigos or Tres Agaves Reposado
  • Flavor it: 0.5 oz mango, strawberry or passion fruit puree

Guest menu: Herradura Blanco Tequila, fresh squeezed lemon & lime, sweet agave

Storm Warning

$14

Glass Pint (16 oz)

Garnish

  • Orange and cherry, speared

Ingredients

  • 0.75 ozParrot Bay Silver rum
  • 0.75 ozParrot Bay Spiced rum
  • 0.75 ozorange juice
  • 0.75 ozcranberry juice
  • 0.75 ozpineapple juice
  • 0.5 ozgrenadine, sunk
  • 0.25 ozDiplomático Dark Rum, float

Build

  1. In an iced shaker, add the rums and juices.
  2. Dirty dump into a pint glass.
  3. Sink the grenadine and float the Diplomático dark rum on top.
  4. Garnish with a speared orange and cherry.

To confirm: Menu lists Red Bull, but the spec has no Red Bull amount. About 4.5 oz of liquid will look short in a 16 oz pint: add a measured top or use the Collins.

Offer the upsell

  • Make it a double (extra rum)

Guest menu: Parrot Bay Silver Rum, Parrot Bay Spiced Rum, tropical pineapple, orange and cranberry juice, Red Bull, Diplomático Dark Rum float

Apple Cider White Sangria

$____
Single serving

Glass Pint (16 oz)

Garnish

  • Mixed IQF berries, in the drink
  • Diced apples on top
  • Orange wheel on the rim

Ingredients

  • 3.875 ozSauvignon Blanc
  • 1.25 ozapple cider
  • 0.625 ozLoggerhead Apple Pie moonshine
  • 0.25 ozcinnamon simple syrup
  • TopSprite

Build

  1. Fill a pint glass with ice.
  2. Add the wine, cider, moonshine and cinnamon syrup (6 oz total). Stir briefly.
  3. Top with Sprite.
  4. Add the defrosted berry mix into the drink.
  5. Top with diced apples and set an orange wheel on the rim.

Batch mix · pre-shift

  • 1.5 LSauvignon Blanc
  • 16 ozapple cider
  • 8 ozLoggerhead Apple Pie moonshine
  • 4 ozcinnamon simple syrup

Makes about 13 drinks. Pour 6 oz of mix per glass, then finish steps 3–5.

Offer the upsell

  • Float 0.5 oz Fireball Cinnamon
